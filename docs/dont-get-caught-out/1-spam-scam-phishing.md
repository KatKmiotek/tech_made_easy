# Spam, Scam or Phishing?

These words are often used together, but they mean slightly different things.

## Spam

Spam is unwanted communication, usually sent to lots of people.

It may simply be annoying advertising.

But spam can also contain scams.

## Scam

A scam is a deliberate attempt to deceive somebody.

The scammer may want:

- money
- bank or card details
- passwords
- personal information
- access to an account
- access to a computer or phone

## Phishing

Phishing is a scam where somebody pretends to be a trusted person or organization.

They may try to make you:

- click a link
- open an attachment
- log in to a fake website
- reveal information
- send money
- provide a security code

---

# Important

Older scam advice often focused on:

- spelling mistakes
- poor grammar
- strange-looking emails
- incorrect branding

Those things can still be clues.

But modern scams can be very professional.

So we should focus on:

> **Pressure, behavior and independent verification — not just appearance.**

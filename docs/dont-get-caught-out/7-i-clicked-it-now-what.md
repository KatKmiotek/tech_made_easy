# I Clicked It… Now What?

First:

> **Do not panic and do not hide it. Acting quickly can make a big difference.**

## I Only Received the Message

- Do not reply.
- Do not click links.
- Do not call numbers in the message.
- Report or block it where appropriate.
- Forward the text to **7726** number ([report text message scam](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/phishing-scams/report-scam-text-message))

## I Clicked the Link but Entered Nothing

- Close the page.
- Do not download anything.
- If something downloaded or installed, run a security scan.
- Be alert to follow-up contact.

Clicking something does not automatically mean disaster.

What happened next matters.

## I Entered a Password

- Change it immediately.
- Change it anywhere else you reused it.
- Turn on two-step verification where possible.
- Check the account for unusual activity.

## I Gave Bank or Card Details

> **Contact your bank immediately using a number you know is genuine.**

Use:

- the number on your card
- your banking app
- the official website
- a bank statement

Do not wait to see what happens.



## I Sent Money

> Don't send money via PayPal (friends & family)

- Contact the bank immediately.
- Tell them you believe you have been scammed.
- Preserve messages, receipts and account details.
- Report the fraud.

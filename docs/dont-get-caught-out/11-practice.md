# Let's Practice!

The more examples you see, the easier it becomes to recognize the warning signs.

Here are a few free online quizzes you can try in your own time.

---

## 🕵️ Are You Scam-Savvy?

**Take Five to Stop Fraud**

A short UK quiz with everyday scam situations, including shopping, payments and suspicious requests.

👉 [Take the Scam-Savvy Quiz](https://quiz.takefive-stopfraud.org.uk/)

---

## 🎣 Can You Spot Phishing?

**Google / Jigsaw**

Look at realistic emails and decide:

> **Phishing or genuine?**

After each example, the quiz explains what you should look for.

👉 [Try the Phishing Quiz](https://phishingquiz.withgoogle.com/)

---

## 🤔 How ScamSceptible Are You Today?

**Take Five to Stop Fraud**

This one is a little different.

It looks at things such as:

* being tired
* feeling stressed
* being distracted
* being in a hurry

These can all make it harder to notice the warning signs of a scam.

👉 [Take the ScamSceptible Test](https://www.takefive-stopfraud.org.uk/scamtest/)

---

# Remember

You don't have to get every answer right.

If a scam catches you out during a quiz, ask:

> **What made it look genuine?**

Then remember:

# PAUSE. CHECK. VERIFY.

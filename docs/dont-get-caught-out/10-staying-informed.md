# Staying Informed

Scams change.

It can help to follow trustworthy sources that report current examples.

## Trading Standards Scotland — ScamShare

Trading Standards Scotland publishes a weekly **ScamShare** bulletin.

It covers current:

- phone scams
- email scams
- online scams
- doorstep scams

Website:

https://www.tsscot.co.uk/news/scam-share/


## Local Facebook Groups

Community groups can also be useful. One I am on is called Scotland Scammers.  

For example:

> "Has anyone else had someone claiming to be from the council today?"

Benefits:

- quick local warnings
- neighbors helping each other
- patterns becoming visible

But remember:

- comments can be wrong
- scammers can also join groups
- fake support accounts may contact people who ask for help

> **Use community groups to become aware.**

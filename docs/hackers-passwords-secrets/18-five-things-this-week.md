We have covered a lot today.

You do not need to change everything at once.

Start with these five things.


## 1. Protect your email account

Make sure your main email has:

- a unique password
- two-step verification
- up-to-date recovery details

<div className="alert alert--success" role="alert">

**If you only improve one account, start with your email.**

</div>

---

## 2. Stop reusing one important password

Think about the accounts that matter most:

- email
- banking
- Facebook
- shopping

Pick one reused password and replace it with something unique.

One change is better than no change.

---

## 3. Check where your passwords are stored

Find out whether your phone is already using:

- Apple Passwords
- Google Password Manager
- Microsoft password storage
- another password manager

Make sure you know how to access it safely.

---

## 4. Turn on two-step verification

Start with your email.

Then consider:

- social media
- shopping
- financial accounts

<div className="alert alert--info" role="alert">

**Remember:**

Something you know

plus

something you have

is much stronger than a password alone.

</div>

---

## 5. Check your recovery options

Look at:

- recovery phone number
- recovery email
- trusted devices
- backup codes

Ask yourself:

### If I lost my phone today, could I still get back into my account?

---

## Bonus check

Look at the devices currently signed into your main email account.

Do you recognize all of them?

If not:

- sign out unfamiliar devices
- change your password
- review your security settings

---

## Keep it simple

You do not need perfect security.

You need better habits.

<div className="alert alert--success" role="alert">

**The goal is not to make the internet complicated.**

The goal is to make it harder for someone else to get into your accounts.

</div>

---

## Your checklist

Before next time, try to complete at least one of these:

- [ ] My email password is unique
- [ ] Two-step verification is turned on
- [ ] I know where my passwords are stored
- [ ] My recovery phone number is correct
- [ ] I checked my signed-in devices

Even one tick is progress.

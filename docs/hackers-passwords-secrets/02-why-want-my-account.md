# Why Would Anyone Want My Account?

A common thought is:

> “Why would a hacker be interested in me?”

The answer is simple:

## Your accounts can be useful

Someone who gets into your account may be able to:

- Read personal messages
- Pretend to be you
- Contact your friends and family
- Ask people for money
- Find personal information about you
- Reset passwords for other accounts
- Use your account to send scams
- Access saved payment details or shopping accounts

---

## Your email is especially important

Your email account is often connected to many other services.

Think about what happens when you click:

**“Forgot your password?”**

Where does the reset link usually go?

👉 To your email.

That means access to your email can sometimes give someone a way into other accounts too.

---

## Social media accounts are valuable too

Imagine someone gets access to your Facebook account.

They could message your friends:

> “Hi, I’m stuck and need some help. Can you send me £50?”

Your friends may trust the message because it appears to come from **you**.

---

## It is not always about money

Sometimes criminals want:

- A trusted account
- Personal information
- Photos
- Contact lists
- Access to other accounts
- An account they can use for scams

Even an old account you rarely use can be useful to someone else.

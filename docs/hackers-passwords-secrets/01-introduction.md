# Hackers, Passwords & Secrets

## What everyone should know

We all use passwords every day.

For email.  
Facebook.  
Shopping.  
Banking.  
Our phones.

But have you ever wondered:

- How do hackers actually get hold of passwords?
- Can a strong password still be stolen?
- Why would anyone want access to **your** account?
- What happens if you use the same password more than once?
- Is Face ID safer than a password?
- And are passwords slowly disappearing?

Today we will look at what really happens behind the scenes when someone tries to get into an account.

We will also look at some real examples, test a few password myths and learn simple things we can all do to stay safer online.

> **No technical knowledge needed — just curiosity.**

# Let's Check Your Device

Now it's time to check your own phone, tablet or computer.

Don't worry if you're not sure where to find something — **ask and we'll look together.**

---

## 1. Is it up to date?

Check whether your device has any updates waiting.

**Windows**

Start → Settings → Windows Update

**iPhone / iPad**

Settings → General → Software Update

**Android**

Search your **Settings** for:

**Software update**

- [ ] My device is up to date
- [ ] Automatic updates are enabled

---

## 2. Is antivirus protection working?

**Windows**

Search for:

**Windows Security**

Then open:

**Virus & threat protection**

Look for any warnings or actions that need your attention.

- [ ] Antivirus protection is active
- [ ] There are no warnings I don't understand

:::tip
If another antivirus program is installed, you might see its name here instead.

Not sure what you're looking at? Ask!
:::

---

## 3. Does your device lock?

Check that your phone, tablet or computer requires something to unlock it.

This could be:

- PIN
- Password
- Fingerprint
- Face recognition

- [ ] My device has a screen lock
- [ ] It locks automatically when I'm not using it

---

## 4. Check your app permissions

Pick a few apps you use regularly.

Check which ones can access your:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Location
- Photos
- Contacts

Ask yourself:

> **Does this app actually need this permission?**

- [ ] I recognize the apps that have access
- [ ] Their permissions make sense

---

## 5. Could you find your phone?

Check whether your device-finding service is enabled.

**Apple**

Look for **Find My**

**Android**

Look for **Find Hub**

- [ ] Device finding is enabled
- [ ] I know which account it uses

---

## 6. Are your important things backed up?

Think especially about:

**Photos, contacts and important documents.**

Check whether they're being saved somewhere other than only on this device.

- [ ] My photos are being backed up or synchronized
- [ ] My important files exist somewhere else too
- [ ] My online storage isn't full

---

# How did your device do?

You don't need every security product or complicated setting.

A well-protected device should be:

- Up to date

- Protected

- Locked

- Backed up

- Used with a little bit of caution

---

:::info Remember
You don't need to understand every technical message your device shows you.

If something unexpected appears:

**Stop → Read → Think before you click.**

And if you're not sure, it's OK to ask someone for help.
:::

# Antivirus — Do I Need It?

This is one of the most common questions about keeping a computer safe.

**The short answer:** If you use a modern Windows computer, you probably already have antivirus protection.

---

## Why did we always need antivirus before?

Years ago, Windows computers had much less security built in.

Viruses could spread through:

- 💾 Floppy disks and USB drives
- 📧 Email attachments
- 🌐 Files downloaded from the internet
- 💿 CDs and copied software
- 🦠 Infected programs

Installing antivirus software such as **Norton, McAfee or AVG** became an essential part of setting up a computer.

> "Never use a computer without antivirus!"

For many years, this was **very good advice**.

---

## What changed?

Modern versions of Windows come with security protection already built in.

### Windows Security includes Microsoft Defender Antivirus

It can:

- Scan files and programs for threats
- Check downloads
- Provide real-time protection
- Automatically receive new security information
- Warn you when something suspicious is detected

And for normal home use, **you don't have to pay extra for it.**

---

## Do I already have it?

On a Windows computer:

**Start → search for "Windows Security"**

Then open:

**Virus & threat protection**

You can check whether your antivirus protection is active.

:::tip
Not sure what protection your computer has?

We'll check it together later!
:::

---

## Do I need to pay for antivirus?

For many home users, **paying for antivirus isn't necessary just to get basic virus protection.**

Paid security packages may include extras such as:

- 🔑 Password managers
- 🌍 VPN services
- ☁️ Cloud backup
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Parental controls
- 🪪 Identity monitoring
- 📞 Technical support

These features can be useful — but that doesn't mean **everyone needs them**.

Before renewing, ask:

> **What am I actually paying for?**

---

## Is more antivirus better?

**No!**

Installing several antivirus programs doesn't give you several layers of protection.

They can interfere with each other, slow your computer down or produce confusing warnings.

### One active, up-to-date antivirus is enough.

---

## Be careful with antivirus warnings

Not every message saying:

> ⚠️ **YOUR COMPUTER HAS 5 VIRUSES!**

is actually from your antivirus.

Websites and adverts can display frightening messages designed to make you:

- Click a link
- Download software
- Pay for unnecessary protection
- Call a fake support number

### Don't panic and don't click straight away.

If you're unsure, close the browser and check **Windows Security** directly.

---

## What about a Mac?

Mac computers also have security protection built in.

macOS includes several layers of protection, including:

- **XProtect** — Apple's built-in protection against known malware
- **Gatekeeper** — checks apps before they are opened
- Security updates provided by Apple
- Warnings when an app comes from an unknown or untrusted developer

You normally don't need to install separate antivirus software just because you have a Mac.

### But Macs can still get malware

You may have heard:

> **"Macs don't get viruses."**

That's not really true.

Macs have strong built-in security, but they can still be affected by malicious software, unsafe downloads and scams.

The same basic rules still apply:

**Keep your Mac updated and be careful about what you download and install.**

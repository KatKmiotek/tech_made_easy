# AI Image Generation

Modern AI tools can create images from written instructions.

This is called **AI image generation**.

You simply describe what you would like to see, and the AI creates an image based on your description.

---

# Examples of What AI Can Create

AI can generate:

- Landscapes
- Animals
- Fantasy scenes
- Posters
- Greeting cards
- Illustrations
- Realistic photographs
- Artistic paintings

Many people now use AI image tools for:
- hobbies
- creative projects
- presentations
- social media
- ideas and inspiration

---

# The Prompt Matters

Just like with AI chat tools:

> Better prompts usually create better images.

Small details can dramatically change the result.

---

# Example 1 — Simple Prompt

## Prompt:
> “Castle”

The AI has very little information, so the result may be generic.

---

# Example 2 — Better Prompt

## Prompt:
> “A realistic Scottish castle beside a misty loch at sunrise”

Now the AI understands:
- location
- atmosphere
- style
- lighting

The image becomes much more detailed and interesting.

---

# Example 3 — Detailed Prompt

## Prompt:
> “A realistic Scottish castle beside a misty loch at sunrise, cinematic lighting, mountains in the background, autumn colours, highly detailed”

Adding extra detail can influence:
- colours
- mood
- realism
- artistic style

---

# You Can Ask for Different Styles

You can describe the style you want.

Examples:

- Watercolor painting
- Pencil sketch
- Cartoon style
- Oil painting
- Realistic photograph
- Vintage postcard

---

# AI Images Are Becoming Common

AI-generated images are now used in:
- advertising
- websites
- presentations
- social media
- news stories

Because of this, it is important to remember:

> Not every image online is necessarily real.

---

# Fun Ways to Try It

You could ask AI to create:

- Your dream holiday location
- A fantasy version of your village
- A pet wearing sunglasses
- A futuristic Scottish landscape
- A personalized birthday card

AI image generation is often one of the easiest and most enjoyable ways to explore AI tools.

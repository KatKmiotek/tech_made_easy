## Chatbot

A chatbot is a computer program designed to have conversations with people.

Examples include:

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Claude
- Customer support chat windows

Modern AI chatbots can answer questions, explain topics, and help with everyday tasks.

---

## Prompt

A **prompt** is the instruction or question you give to AI.

Examples:

- “Explain this in simple terms”
- “Help me plan a holiday”
- “Create an image of a Scottish castle”

The better the prompt, the better the result usually becomes.

---

## Generative AI

Generative AI means AI that creates new content.

It can generate:

- Text
- Images
- Music
- Videos

Examples:
- AI writing an email
- AI creating artwork
- AI generating ideas

---

## Algorithm

An algorithm is a set of instructions a computer follows.

Algorithms help decide:

- What videos appear on YouTube
- Which posts you see on Facebook
- What adverts appear online

Many websites use algorithms to personalize content.

---

## Hallucination

Sometimes AI gives incorrect information while sounding very confident.

This is called an **AI hallucination**.

Important reminder:

> AI can sound convincing even when it is wrong.

This is why important information should always be checked carefully.

---

## Deepfake

A deepfake is a fake image, video, or voice created using AI.

Deepfakes can sometimes look very realistic.

This is why it is important to:
- double-check information
- be cautious online
- avoid trusting shocking stories immediately

---

## Machine Learning

Machine learning is a way of teaching computers by showing them many examples.

Instead of following only fixed rules, the computer learns patterns from data.

Examples:
- Spam email filters
- Netflix recommendations
- Voice recognition
- AI assistants

The more examples the system analyses, the better it usually becomes.

---

## Training Data

Training data is the information AI learns from.

This can include:
- books
- articles
- websites
- images

The quality of the training data affects how accurate and useful the AI becomes.

---

## Cloud

The “cloud” means files or services stored on computers connected through the internet rather than only on your own device.

Examples include:
- Google Drive
- iCloud
- OneDrive

Many AI tools also run in the cloud.

---

# Key Message

You do not need to become an AI expert.

But understanding a few common AI terms can help you:
- feel more confident
- understand the news more easily
- use AI tools more effectively
- stay safer online

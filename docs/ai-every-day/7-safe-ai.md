# Staying Safe With AI

AI tools can be very useful, but it is important to use them safely and responsibly.

Like any online service, AI tools should be used with common sense and caution.

---

# Be Careful With Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive or private information with AI tools.

Examples include:

- Passwords
- Bank details
- Medical information
- Private documents
- Confidential work information

A good rule is:

> Do not type anything you would not feel comfortable sharing online.

---

# Does AI Learn From Conversations?

Some AI services may use conversations to improve their systems and future versions.

Many tools now offer:
- privacy settings
- chat history controls
- options to disable training

Privacy policies differ between companies, so it is important to understand the service you are using.

---

# Free vs Paid AI Services

Some AI tools offer both free and paid versions.

Paid versions may provide:
- more features
- faster responses
- higher usage limits
- improved privacy controls

Business and professional versions often include stronger privacy protections than free public tools.

---

# Important Reminder

Even when using paid AI services:

- always read privacy settings carefully
- avoid sharing highly sensitive information
- remember that different companies have different policies

Paying for AI does not automatically mean complete privacy.

---

# AI Is Not Always Correct

AI can sometimes:
- misunderstand questions
- provide outdated information
- generate incorrect answers confidently

This is why important information should always be checked carefully.

Especially for:
- health advice
- financial decisions
- legal matters
- important purchases

---

# Watch Out for Scams and Fake Content

AI can now create:
- fake images
- fake videos
- fake voices
- fake news stories

These can sometimes look very convincing.

Be especially careful if something:
- seems shocking
- creates urgency
- asks for money or personal details
- appears “too good to be true”

---

# Use Trusted AI Tools

When possible:
- use well-known AI services
- download apps from official app stores
- avoid suspicious websites claiming to offer AI tools

Some fake AI apps are designed to:
- collect personal data
- show misleading adverts
- scam users

---

# Remember That AI Is a Tool

AI can assist people, but it should not replace:
- critical thinking
- human judgement
- trusted professional advice

Think of AI as:
> A helpful assistant, not a perfect expert.

---

# Key Message

You do not need to fear AI.

But understanding:
- its strengths
- its limitations
- and basic online safety

can help you use AI more confidently and responsibly.

# What AI Is NOT

There are many exciting stories about AI in the news, but also a lot of confusion and misinformation.

It is important to understand both the strengths and limitations of AI tools.

---

# AI Is Not Human

AI can sound very natural and convincing, but it does not:

- Think like a person
- Have emotions
- Understand meaning the way humans do
- Have opinions or common sense

AI predicts likely answers based on patterns in data.

---

# AI Is Not Always Correct

AI tools sometimes give incorrect information while sounding very confident.

This can happen because:

- Information may be outdated
- AI misunderstood the question
- AI generated an incorrect answer

This is why important information should always be checked carefully.

Especially for:

- Health advice
- Financial decisions
- Legal matters
- Important personal information

---

# AI Is Not Magic

AI can be very helpful, but it still has limitations.

It cannot:

- Replace human experience
- Make important life decisions for you
- Know everything
- Guarantee correct answers

Think of AI as:

> A helpful assistant, not an all-knowing expert.

---

# Not Everything Online Is Real

AI can now create:

- Fake images
- Fake videos
- Fake voices
- Fake news stories
- Can fake job interviews

These are sometimes called **deepfakes**.

Because of this, it is becoming more important to:

- Double-check information
- Be careful with scams
- Avoid trusting shocking stories immediately

---

# Common AI Myths

| Myth | Reality |
|---|---|
| “AI knows everything” | AI can make mistakes |
| “AI is always truthful” | AI sometimes generates false information |
| “Only experts can use AI” | Anyone can learn the basics |
| “AI will replace everyone’s job tomorrow” | AI is more likely to change jobs than completely replace people |

---

# Key Message

AI is a powerful tool, but it should be used with:

- Common sense
- Careful thinking
- Human judgement

The goal is not to fear AI or blindly trust it — but to understand it and use it wisely.
